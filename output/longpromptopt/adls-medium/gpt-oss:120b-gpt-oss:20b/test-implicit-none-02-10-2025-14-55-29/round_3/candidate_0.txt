The activities of daily living (ADLs) is a term used to collectively describe fundamental skills required to independently care for oneself, 
such as eating, bathing, and mobility. These include personal hygiene or grooming, ability to dress independently, ability to toilet independently,
ability to transfer/ambulate independently, and ability to eat on one's own/feed oneself.

Given the patient note, determine whether the patient is independent or dependent in the specified ADL type.

The output should be in the following format as a JSON object (DON'T GENERATE ANYTHING ELSE):

{
    "reasoning": This should be a string with the reasoning for your classification. This should include the specific parts of the patient note that you used to make your classification.

    "classification": This should be 'yes' if the patient is independent in the ADL, 'no' if the patient is dependent in the ADL, or 'no info' if there isn't enough information.
}

RULES TO FOLLOW:
1. If a patient is able to perform an ADL with the help of a device (e.g., walker, shower chair), they are considered independent in that ADL.
2. If a patient is able to ambulate independently, they are independent in the Transferring ADL
3. Be wary of the patient note referencing independence or dependence in ADLs in general without mentioning a specific one. If it says something like "all ADLs," you can extend the statement to the ADL you are classifying. However, if it says "some ADLs" but doesn't say what that "some" is, you can't extend the statement to the ADL you are classifying.
4. Not remembering to do an ADL on a daily life basis implies dependence in that ADL.
5. If the note mentions independence in a specific set of ADLs, you can assume dependence in the other ADLs (unless there is an explicit statement to the contrary). Similarly, if the note mentions dependence in a specific set of ADLs, you can assume independence in the other ADLs (unless there is an explicit statement to the contrary).
6. Please take a stance of "no info unless told otherwise." If the note does not explicitly state independence or dependence in the ADL (this includes discussing the ADL without using the term 'ADL') and/or doesn't make a clear statement about ADLs in general, classify it as "no info". You should NOT infer independence or dependence based on your medical knowledge and the patient's general condition during/after treatment, the details of their treatment, etc.
7. In general, don't overthink or over-infer. A human should be able to pick up on any ADL independence or dependence from a relatively quick scan of the note and without any logical jumps/overinference. Otherwise, it is likely "no info."
